## 🌿 Overview

**HEALTH PLUS** connects daily nutrition, physical activity, and historical lifestyle habits into a continuous, explainable feedback loop. Rather than functioning as an isolated calorie tracker, a disconnected workout counter, or an open-ended generic chatbot, HEALTH PLUS identifies meaningful patterns in user-confirmed wellness data over time.

### The Core Triad
1. **Observe:** Collect user-confirmed food logs, macro/micronutrient estimates, and computer-vision-assisted exercise performance metrics.
2. **Understand:** Analyze historical records through statistical baseline modeling, identify meaningful behavioral and physiological patterns over time, and compare activity against personal baselines.
3. **Guide:** Provide contextual, explainable, and actionable suggestions through an AI-powered personal coach and dynamic diet/workout planners.

## 🛡️ Strict Boundaries & Safety

- **No Medical Prescriptions:** HEALTH PLUS provides wellness and lifestyle guidance; it does not replace medical doctors or physiotherapists.
- **Zero Fabricated Statistics:** Missing logs are treated as unrecorded data, not assumed zeros or negative user behavior.
- **Privacy First:** Video camera streams never leave the user's browser; food photographs are ephemeral and discarded after recognition unless explicitly saved.
- **Row-Level Security:** Multi-tenant PostgreSQL isolation guarantees users only access their verified data.
